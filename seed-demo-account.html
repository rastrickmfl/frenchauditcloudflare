Seed the "test" demo account

This one-time utility populates the test pupil account with randomly-rated vocab, two personal vocab sets, a custom AQA set, and three teacher-shared vocab lists (Adjectives, Colours, Numbers 1-100) targeted at the "test" account — so you can log in as test and demo the app to pupils.

It's safe to run more than once — it will refresh the "test" account's data and replace just its own three demo teacher lists (any other real teacher lists you've already created are left untouched).

This page only works once it's deployed alongside the app (it calls the same /api/... endpoints as the app itself, from the same site) — it won't work opened as a local file. Once you're done demoing, feel free to delete this file from the repo; it isn't linked from anywhere in the app.
